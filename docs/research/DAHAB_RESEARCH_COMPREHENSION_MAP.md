## 1. Executive Research Synthesis: What the Corpus Knows

The Dahab research corpus comprises 36 specialized, field-audited, and regulatory documents spanning 26 operational domains. Rather than a tourist brochure, the corpus represents an **empirical operational model** of a low-rise, coastal Bedouin fishing settlement turned global marine sports and digital nomad capital in the South Sinai Governorate of Egypt (28.5097° N, 34.5136° E).

### Core Macro Realities Understood:
1. **Micro-Spatial Layout & Pedestrian Core**: Dahab is organized across 17 distinct micro-zones anchored around a 2.8 km continuous pedestrian promenade (Lighthouse, Masbat, Mashraba) extending north to Eel Garden and south to Laguna, with outer expedition corridors requiring dedicated transport (Blue Hole, Ras Abu Galum, Blue Lagoon, Wadi Gnai, St. Catherine).
2. **Marine Geography & Reef Invariants**: Unlike boat-heavy destinations (Hurghada/Sharm), Dahab is fundamentally a **shore-entry** diving and snorkeling destination with fragile fringing reef shelves, strict entry corridors (Sandy Channel at Eel Garden, Bell Hole at Blue Hole, Wide Gravel at Lighthouse), and zero-tolerance environmental mandates (no glove diving, reef-walking ban).
3. **Wind Dynamics & Micro-Protection**: The Gulf of Aqaba funnel creates steady NNE thermal wind regimes (>300 days/year). While high winds (>20 knots) create dangerous chop on outer northern reef walls (Blue Hole, Canyon), the headland at Lighthouse Bay provides leeward calm water suitable for beginners, while Laguna's sand spit creates world-class flat-water high-speed training corridors.
4. **Bedouin Stewardship & Local Logistics**: Land transport, mountain treks, camel journeys, and desert camps are operated through the Muzeina and Tarabin Bedouin tribal guilds. Commercial viability requires direct, respectful integration (prompt electronic/voucher payouts, certified local guides) rather than third-party agency markups.
5. **Hyperbaric & Critical Safety**: Multiplace recompression chamber infrastructure operates 24/7 at Dahab General Hospital (Kilo 2). Scuba diving enforces the mandatory 24-hour no-fly and no-mountain-ascent interval.

## 3. Epistemic Classification: Dissecting Fact, Observation, Rule, and Inferences

To ensure the knowledge system never conflates field observations with authoritative truth, the corpus is decomposed across 6 epistemic tiers:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. AUTHORITATIVE TRUTH (Decrees, CDWS limits, Coordinates, 24h No-Fly) │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. VERIFIED OPERATIONAL (Licensed Suppliers, Base SKUs, Fleet SLAs)     │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. TEMPORAL / SEASONAL (Monthly Temp, Water Temp, Wind Corridors)      │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. OBSERVATIONS & SIGNALS (Field Prices, Menu Items, Walkability)       │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 5. RULES & CONSTRAINTS (Tide windows, Wetsuit mm, Permit Gates)        │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 6. INFERENCES & RECOMMENDATIONS (Persona affinity, 2-Hour recommendations)│
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Examples of Epistemic Rigor:
- **Authoritative Fact**: The Blue Hole Arch sits at 56 meters depth and is strictly prohibited for single-tank recreational scuba diving under CDWS regulations.
- **Operational Fact**: Red Sea Relax Diving Center holds license `CDWS-DHB-104` and maintains an inventory of 40 scuba sets and 12 certified instructors.
- **Temporal Fact**: Average Red Sea water temperature at Lighthouse in August is 27.5°C–28.5°C; in February it is 21.0°C–22.0°C.
- **Observation**: A field audit at Ali Baba Restaurant observed a Catch of the Day seafood dinner averaging 480 EGP per person.
- **Rule / Constraint**: Divers completing multiple deep dives cannot participate in the Mount Sinai sunrise trek or take a flight from SSH for 24 hours post-dive.
- **Recommendation**: For a traveler at Lighthouse with a 2-hour window and a "relaxed" pace preference, recommend a 1-hour Shore Snorkel at Lighthouse Reef followed by seafood at Ali Baba.

## 4. Conflict Analysis & Resolution Framework

The research identifies several potential epistemic and operational tensions that must be resolved systematically:

1. **Price Variance (Field Observation vs Supplier SKU)**:
   - *Conflict*: Field audits report Try Dives at 1,200 EGP while high-season boutique resorts quote 1,600 EGP.
   - *Resolution*: Classify observed rates as `PRICING_OBSERVATION` with `validFrom/validUntil` ranges; authoritative production pricing is determined exclusively by certified `ProductSkuEntity.basePriceEGP`.
2. **Eel Garden Accessibility (Static Open vs Tide-Dependent)**:
   - *Conflict*: General guides list Eel Garden as "open 24/7", but marine biology confirms the reef flat is dangerously impassable during extreme low spring tide.
   - *Resolution*: Model Eel Garden with a dynamic `ConditionConstraint`: `tideState != 'EXTREME_LOW_SPRING'`.
3. **Transport Booking (Instant Hail vs Pre-Arranged Transfer)**:
   - *Conflict*: City taxis are street-hail cash only, while SSH Airport transfers require pre-dispatch.
   - *Resolution*: Split transport into distinct products: `trans-city-bedouin` (`WALK_IN` / `HAIL`) vs `trans-ssh-private` (`BOOK_NOW` with 2h SLA).
