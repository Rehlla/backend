## 2. Mandatory Diver & Freediver Safety Invariants
1. **The 24-Hour No-Fly Rule**: Scuba divers completing multiple dives or deep dives (Blue Hole / Canyon) **MUST NOT** board flights from Sharm El Sheikh (SSH) or ascend above 300m elevation (St. Catherine Mountain Trek) for at least 18–24 hours post-dive.
2. **The 130m Blue Hole Arch Rule**: The Arch is strictly forbidden for recreational single-tank scuba divers (capped at 40m). Commercial dive centers enforce CDWS check-in registers.
3. **Hyperbaric Chamber Support**: Dahab has a dedicated Hyperbaric Chamber facility located at Dahab General Medical Hospital with 24/7 on-call diving medicine physicians (`+20 69 364 0535`).
4. **Freediving Safety Protocol**: Zero solo freediving policy. All depth sessions at Blue Hole or Lighthouse require certified buddy system, lanyard, and surface safety float with counterballast system.
