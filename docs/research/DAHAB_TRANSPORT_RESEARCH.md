## 2. Checkpoints & Regulatory Transit Invariants
1. **Dahab City Entrance Checkpoint (Kilo 16 Gate)**:
   - Mandatory security checkpoint for all incoming vehicles from Sharm El Sheikh or St. Catherine.
   - Foreign travelers must have passport with valid Egyptian entry stamp/Sinai Only permit.
   - Egyptian nationals must present national ID.
2. **Blue Hole Environmental Gate**:
   - South Sinai Protected Area entry ticket: $10 USD (or equivalent in EGP) for foreign tourists, 50 EGP for Egyptian nationals. Open from 07:00 AM to 05:00 PM daily.
