### Persona 1: Ahmed – The High-Pace Cairo Urban Escapee (First/Second Timer)
- **Profile**: 29–36 year old tech manager/entrepreneur from New Cairo or Sheikh Zayed. Takes Thursday 06:00 PM flight to SSH or Thursday night sleeper bus.
- **Dahab Goals**: Decompress instantly, try beginner scuba diving or snorkeling, eat authentic grilled seafood at Ali Baba, sunset tea at Mount Twilat, zero logistical friction.
- **Pain Points**: Time-poor, hates waiting for taxis, hates aggressive street hawkers, wants instant bookable reliability and card payments.
- **Language**: Egyptian Arabic native, fluent English.

### Persona 2: Sarah – The Global Digital Nomad & Freediver
- **Profile**: 27–34 year old remote UX designer or software engineer from Europe/North America staying 3 to 8 weeks.
- **Dahab Goals**: Morning freediving line training at Lighthouse (07:30 AM), fast fiber Wi-Fi afternoon deep work, evening social sunset stroll.
- **Pain Points**: Flaky hotel Wi-Fi, unexpected power outages, hidden card transaction fees, needs reliable community and healthy food.
- **Language**: English native.

### Persona 3: Marco & Elena – The Certified Technical Scuba Explorers
- **Profile**: 32–45 year old European certified Advanced Open Water / Trimix divers.
- **Dahab Goals**: Canyon chasm dive, Bells to Blue Hole drift, Ras Abu Galum boat safari, meticulous dive computer logbook tracking.
- **Pain Points**: Strict adherence to no-fly times, gear drying space, unorganized dive schedules, safety gas blends.
- **Language**: English / Italian / German.

### Persona 4: Layla & Friends – The Budget Cultured Sinai Road-Trippers
- **Profile**: 22–28 year old Egyptian university graduates or creative freelancers.
- **Dahab Goals**: Budget-conscious, authentic Bedouin camp in Blue Lagoon, falafel breakfast at Yum Yum, cruiser bikes along promenade.
- **Pain Points**: High markups, rigid tour packages, wants genuine Bedouin connections and transparent pricing.
- **Language**: Egyptian Arabic native.
