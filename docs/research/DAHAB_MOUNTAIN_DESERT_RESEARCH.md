## 2. Invariant Safety Rules for Desert Treks
1. **Diving Interval Rule**: No ascending to St. Catherine or High Sinai Canyons within 24 hours of completing deep scuba dives.
2. **Flash Flood Protocol (*Sayl*)**: In rare winter rain events (November–February), all wadi expeditions are immediately cancelled by South Sinai Police due to dangerous flash torrents.
3. **Connectivity Reality**: Zero cellular coverage exists inside White Canyon and deep Wadi Qnai; offline GPS waypoints and Bedouin guide radios are mandatory.
