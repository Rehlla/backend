## 1. Wind Regimes & Micro-Corridors

```
      Gulf of Aqaba Funnel (Sinai Mountains vs Saudi Mountains)
             ↓↓↓↓↓↓ North-Northeast Prevailing Wind Corridor (NNE) ↓↓↓↓↓↓
  [Blue Hole: High Swell / Windward] 
       → [Lighthouse: Leeward Protected Bay / Gentle Water] 
            → [Laguna: Open Wind Acceleration Funnel / 15-28 Knots Flat Water]
```

- **Prevailing Direction**: North-Northeast (NNE) thermal gradient funneling down the Gulf of Aqaba.
- **Wind Acceleration**: Peak wind speed occurs between 11:00 AM and 04:30 PM due to inland desert thermal heating.
- **Micro-Protection Zones**:
  - **Lighthouse Bay**: Protected by the headland; remains calm for snorkeling and beginner dives even during 20-knot winds.
  - **Laguna**: Natural sandbar provides glass-flat water inside the spit with 18–25 knot clean cross-shore winds (world-class kitesurf/windsurf training).
  - **Blue Hole / Canyon**: Exposed to northern open sea chop; waves break heavily against the outer reef saddle when winds exceed 18 knots.

## 3. Marine Disruption Rules
- **Wind > 22 Knots**: Trigger automatic warning for Blue Hole boat transfers and northern outer reef shore entries; redirect divers to Lighthouse or Southern sheltered bays (Bannerfish Bay, Moray Garden).
- **Extreme High UV (Index > 10)**: Midday sun exposure warnings (11:30 AM – 03:00 PM); enforce hydration (min. 3L water/day for divers and desert trekkers).
