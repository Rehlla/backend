## 2. Micro-Spatial Constraints & Physical Bottlenecks
1. **Promenade Walkability**: The continuous seafront strip runs from the south of Mashraba to the northern tip of Eel Garden (~2.8 km). It is 100% pedestrian-friendly, with no motor vehicles permitted between 10:00 AM and 02:00 AM.
2. **High-Tide vs Low-Tide Channels**:
   - **Eel Garden**: Entry is through a narrow natural reef canyon (depth 1.5m). At extreme low spring tide (new/full moon), crossing the reef flat is hazardous to corals and divers. Recommended entry: Mid-to-High tide only.
   - **Lighthouse**: 100% accessible at all tide states due to wide natural gravel bay.
3. **Expedition Mode Transition Threshold**:
   - Travel north beyond Blue Hole into Ras Abu Galum or Blue Lagoon immediately triggers `Expedition Mode` (no ATM, no grid power, offline cache required, cash only, satellite/emergency contact mandatory).
