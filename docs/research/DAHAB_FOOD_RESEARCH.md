## 2. Dietary & Timing Rules for Divers & Nomads
1. **Pre-Dive Breakfast Window**: Divers must complete breakfast at least 60–90 minutes before a 08:30 AM morning dive. Ralph's Bakery opens at 07:00 AM specifically to serve early divers.
2. **Post-Dive Salt Rehydration**: Post-dive meals benefit from fresh electrolytes (Fresh mango/lemon-mint juices at Lighthouse cafes) and mineral-rich fresh salads.
3. **Bedouin Mountain Dinners**: Traditional desert dinners in Wadi Qnai or Mount Twilat feature *Zarb* (underground pit-roasted lamb/chicken with spiced rice, slow-cooked for 4 hours beneath hot coals). Requires min. 3 hours advance request.
