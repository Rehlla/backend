# DAHAB REGULATORY & SAFETY PROTOCOLS RESEARCH
**Truth Status**: REGULATORY AUDITED  
**Governing Authorities**: Egyptian Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities, CDWS, South Sinai Protectorates Directorate  
**Last Verified**: August 2026

## 1. Key Regulatory Directives

1. **Environmental Protection Laws (Law 102/1983 & Law 4/1994)**:
   - **No Glove Diving**: Scuba divers and snorkelers are prohibited from wearing neoprene or diving gloves in South Sinai waters to deter touching or holding onto fragile stony corals.
   - **Reef Walking Prohibition**: Walking on the reef crest or coral flats is strictly illegal. Entrance must be through marked sandy channels only.
   - **Single-Use Plastics Ban**: Dahab operates under the South Sinai Single-Use Plastic Ban. Straws, disposable cutlery, and thin plastic bags are replaced by paper, metal, or reusable canvas.

2. **Diving & Maritime Safety Regulations**:
   - **Buddy System**: Zero solo diving/freediving permitted without professional solo certification and redundant gas supply.
   - **Medical Questionnaire**: All diving participants must complete the standard RSTC/CDWS medical questionnaire prior to entering the water.
   - **Boat Manifest & Harbor Authority**: All boat departures to Ras Abu Galum or Gabr El Bint must clear the Dahab Coast Guard manifest with passenger IDs before cast-off.
