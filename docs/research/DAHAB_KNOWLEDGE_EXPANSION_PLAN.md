# DAHAB KNOWLEDGE EXPANSION PLAN
## Systematic Knowledge Scaling Framework for the Dahab Knowledge Operating System (DKOS)

**Plan Version:** 2.0.0-SCALE  
**Target Authority:** Knowledge Operations & Cartographic Intelligence Guild  
**Execution Objective:** Expand Dahab entity, fact, and operational knowledge breadth and depth without compromising the certified trust architecture.

---

### 1. Expansion Philosophy & Core Directives

1. **Measurable Coverage, Not Illusory Completeness**: Rather than chasing a vague "100% of Dahab", we establish deterministic, verifiable discovery grids with clear spatial boundaries and category completeness scores.
2. **Preserve Trust & Provenance Architecture**: Every added fact must trace directly to a verified source registry ID or an on-the-ground operational field audit. Unverified observations remain quarantined in staging.
3. **Knowledge Density over Shallow Inventory**: We optimize for **facts per entity** (location, tidal access, difficulty, booking mode, freshness, payment methods, food adjacency, emergency protocols) rather than merely amassing name/address stubs.
4. **Demand-Driven Prioritization**: Knowledge expansion is guided by traveler question frequency, commercial actionability, and safety criticality.

### 5. Google Places (New) Discovery & Compliance Strategy

1. **Observation Role Exclusively**: Google Places API (New) data serves exclusively for entity candidate discovery, geocoding validation, and address cross-referencing.
2. **Zero Scraping Policy**: Absolutely no web scraping, HTML scraping, or reverse engineering of Google services.
3. **Field Mask Discipline**: Queries utilize strict field masks (`places.id`, `places.displayName`, `places.location`, `places.primaryType`, `places.formattedAddress`) to prevent quota bloat.
4. **Attribution & Provenance**: Discovered entities carry `sourceType: 'GOOGLE_MAPS'`, requiring corroboration with local ground audits before receiving `AUTHORITATIVE` status.
