## 2. Critical Accommodation Constraints
1. **Early Arrival Reality**: Overnight buses from Cairo arrive in Dahab between 05:30 AM and 07:30 AM. Standard hotel check-in is 14:00. The platform must provide an automated **"Early Arrival Buffer Journey"** (luggage drop + breakfast at Ralph's Bakery + morning snorkel at Lighthouse) so travelers are never stranded.
2. **Wi-Fi Variance**: Promenade beachfront properties often experience fluctuating 4G router speeds; nomad-specific properties utilize dedicated high-speed fiber lines.
