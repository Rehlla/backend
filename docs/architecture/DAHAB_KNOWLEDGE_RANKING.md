## 1. Ranking Dimensions

Every recommendation generated by DKOS is scored on a 0–100 scale computed from 7 transparent, deterministic dimensions:

$$\text{FinalScore} = w_1 S_{\text{geo}} + w_2 S_{\text{time}} + w_3 S_{\text{pace}} + w_4 S_{\text{avail}} + w_5 S_{\text{fresh}} + w_6 S_{\text{supplier}} + w_7 S_{\text{comm}}$$

1. **Geographic Proximity ($S_{\text{geo}}$)**: Distance penalty based on origin zone and walking radius.
2. **Time-Window Fit ($S_{\text{time}}$)**: Penalizes activities whose (Duration + Transit + Buffer) exceeds available minutes.
3. **Traveler Pace Fit ($S_{\text{pace}}$)**: Matches activity intensity against traveler profile (`Relaxed`, `Balanced`, `Active`).
4. **Availability & Booking State ($S_{\text{avail}}$)**: Direct instant bookability yields higher readiness than request-later.
5. **Freshness & Confidence ($S_{\text{fresh}}$)**: Favors recently verified facts and high-authority evidence.
6. **Supplier Certification Tier ($S_{\text{supplier}}$)**: Tier 1 Certified partners receive baseline commercial reliability priority.
7. **Next Booking Compatibility ($S_{\text{comm}}$)**: Verifies zero conflict with existing Day/Trip commitments.
